# Office hours

Beves Office Hours is a Slack bot for quick Q&A.

- **How to ask:** @mention the bot in a channel, or send it a DM.
- **What it covers:** onboarding basics, common tools, and lightweight process notes seeded in this repo under `knowledge/`.
- **What it does not cover:** HR exceptions, legal advice, production credentials, or private customer data.
- **Follow-ups:** Reply in the same Slack thread after mentioning the bot so the conversation stays in one session.
