# Onboarding (sample)

Welcome notes for new teammates — replace with your real checklist.

1. Join the `#general` and `#eng` Slack channels.
2. Set up SSO and your laptop profile.
3. Clone team repos you need; ask your buddy which ones matter first.
4. Read the tools note for the default stack.
5. Book a 30-minute intro with your manager in the first week.

If something is blocked (access, hardware, accounts), post in `#help` or DM your buddy.
